Recommended & Not Recommended Modpacks

We generally suggest users to use popular and good modpacks such as "Pojavly Optimized", "Adrenaline", "Fabulously optimized", "Speed of light", "Simply Optimized forks", "Additive", or "OptiDroid".


We will not provide support to AI generated modpacks, such as:

  • Spunky Optimized;
  • Spunky Boost;
  • Spunky X Vulkan;
  • OptiFPS;
  • RKMC's FPS Boost Modpacks;
  • OptiMobile.

We don't recommend users to use modpacks from Spunky Insaan, RKMC_GAMERZ & DNA Mobile Gaming, since all of their modpacks are AI generated and/or bloated and filled with misinformation.

Information About Renderers

Holy GL4ES

  • Simple Engine, Best Performance;
  • Little Compatibility with Mods & Shaders;
  • Cannot Run Minecraft 1.21.6+.

LTW

  • Almost as fast as GL4ES;
  • Good Compatibility with lots of mods & shaders;
  • Heals Skin Cancer;
  • Supports Minecraft 1.17- Latest.

Zink

  • Very Good Compatibility with almost all Shaders & Mods;
  • Very Slow;
  • Supports Minecraft 1.0- 1.21.5.

Krypton Wrapper

  • Capability to Handle More Advanced Shaders;
  • Capability to Run Minecraft in Almost all Versions;
  • More Advanced OpenGL Features;
  • Run Sodium in Minecraft 1.16.5;
  • Supports Minecraft 1.0- Latest.

MobileGlues

  • Almost as fast as GL4ES;
  • Good Compatibility with lots of Mods like Sodium & Some Shaders;
  • Heals Skin Cancer;
  • Supports Minecraft 1.17- Latest.

Commonly Asked Mods That Doesn't Works

Discord-RPC:

Does not work due to missing library for d-rpc, will be fixed in the future.

Valkyrien Skies:

Not compatible for android and never will be because it is made for PC.

Replay Mod:

Works only if you have FFMpeg plugin installed. Alternatively you can use Record-able.

Flashback:

Does not work due to imgui & aggressive openGL calls. For alternative you can use Record-able.

Minecraft Clients:

Clients like Feather Client (also known as Dawn Client), Fast Client etc. will not work because you can't run a client inside a launcher.

TopkaVisuals:

Uses a VMProtected .dll, doesn't properly work even on x86_64 Windows.

Immersive Portals:

Only works if your native OpenGLES driver has GL_EXT_clip_cull_distance extension supported.

Xareo's Map Mods:

Colours might be incorrect and graphical issues may occur with LTW Renderer.
