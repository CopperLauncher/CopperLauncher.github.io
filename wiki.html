Installation

Here's How to install Copper on Your Android Device! Follow the Steps Below Carefully:

  1. Download the latest prebuilt app from Github Releases.
  2. Install it.
  3. Once done, create an account by clicking the "+ Add Account" button at the top-left corner of your screen.
  4. If you own a "Minecraft: Java Edition" account, it is highly recommended to login with your "Microsoft account"; if you don't have one, only then login with "Local Account".
  5. To create a fresh new vanilla instance, tap "^" icon at the bottom- right, which will open the "Select Instance" menu, then tap "+ Create New Profile" button. then select "Vanilla Like Profile". Give your instance a name & a icon if you want. If you want to play a specific minecraft version, click on the "latest-releaee" box, then select your desired version. Hit "Save".
  6. Click "Play" & wait for it to finish downloading all necessary files.

Modding

Here is the easiest way you can mod your game under 2 minutes, without leaving the launcher!

Installing Mods:

  1. Click on the "Browse Content" button on the main menu.
  2. Click the "Mods" button.
  3. Search your Mod.
  4. Once you find it, click on it & hit "Install".
  5. Make sure the mod version matches with the minecraft version you have installed, otherwise your game will crash!
  6. For managing mods, click "Manege Content" & select "Mods" on the main menu.

Installing Shaders:

  1. Click on the "Browse Content" button on the main menu.
  2. Click on the "Shader Packs" button.
  3. Search your Shaderpack.
  4. Once you find it, click on it & hit "Install".
  5. Make sure you have "Sodium" & "Iris" installed in your mods folder.
  6. For managing shaders, click "Manege Content" & select "Shader Packs" on the main menu.

Installing Resource Packs:

  1. Click on the "Browse Content" button on the main menu.
  2. Click on the "Resource Packs" button.
  3. Search your Resource Pack.
  4. Once you find it, click on it & hit "Install".
  5. For managing resource packs, click "Manege Content" & select "Resource Packs" on the main menu.
